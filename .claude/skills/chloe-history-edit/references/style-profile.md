## Format (consistent across sources) — moderate
- A modern woman (tattoos, crop top, jeans) vlogging handheld selfie-style inside famous historical
  events: Black Death, Pompeii, Titanic, Tudor London, Salem, Wild West, London 1965, Ancient Rome.
- Hook: the flat premise line "I travelled to <place> during <event>", plus a matching caption.
- Voice: a modern Gen-Z influencer reviewing the past; slang; energetic, turning somber.
- Some reels have her warning people of the time about the disaster that's coming.
- 15–70 s. Short scenes, hard cuts, a consistent character across episodes.
