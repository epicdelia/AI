### Tweet 2
1/ Task #1: Boilerplate & Unit Tests -> GONE.

### Tweet 3
2/ Task #2: Debugging Distributed Outages -> HELPS, BUT NEVER LEADS.

When a microservice fails under high concurrency, LLMs hallucinate plausible reasons that have nothing to do with your real infra.

AI doesn't have the context of your legacy database locks. Human intuition remains mandatory.

• Can you design deterministic interfaces?
• Can you stop agents from over-engineering?
