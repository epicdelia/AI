# Barghain — Bouncer Simulator

A tiny choose-your-path mini-game about the hardest door in techno. Make the
right calls in the queue — outfit, group size, phone etiquette, and what you
say to the bouncer — and maybe, just maybe, you get in.

## How to play

Open `index.html` in any browser. No build, no dependencies.

- Every choice moves your hidden **vibe meter** up or down.
- At the door, the bouncer weighs your vibe plus a dose of pure randomness,
  because nobody truly knows the rules.
- Three outcomes: **EINLASS** (you're in), **HEUTE NICHT** (not today), and
  the dreaded flat **NEIN**.

## Unofficial field guide (encoded in the game)

- Wear black. Worn black. Not trying-too-hard black.
- Come alone or with one friend, not with Dave's birthday party.
- Know at least one name on tonight's lineup.
- Phone away. No selfies, no stories, no geotags.
- "Zwei." Eye contact. Nothing else.
