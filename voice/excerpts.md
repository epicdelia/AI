## Patterns to copy (not rules, just what she does)

- Opens with a hedge or aside before the claim ("It's a stretch.. but let's hear it out").
- Punctures her own hype right after it ("I wouldn't tattoo them on my arm yet").
- Explains with one concrete metaphor, then stops (four doors, can't invent a fifth).
- Short standalone lines for the punchline. Trailing "lol…" and "..". The occasional 💀 or 😅 at the end of a line.
- Credits sources and flags when numbers are the company's own.
- Jokes land on tech culture and herself, never on the viewer.
