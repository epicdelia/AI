## Email Body:
Hey Unicorns! 💕

Something wild happened this week: I found an old task journal from my time as a Software Engineer at Google.

Back when I worked across Gmail and Cloud Solutions, my weeks were packed with code reviews, design docs, unit tests, and firefighting customer outages.

So I did an experiment: I drew three columns on my studio whiteboard:
• GONE (AI handles it end-to-end)
• HELPS (great assistant, dangerous without supervision)
• WORTH MORE (skills that doubled in value)

The results surprised me.

Yes, boilerplate and unit tests are completely gone. If you're spending your afternoon hand-typing repetitive test mocks, you're wasting precious mental energy.

But the real insight is what was left in column 3: System Intuition and Verification.
Because anyone can generate 500 lines of code in 10 seconds, the new bottleneck is knowing whether those 500 lines are secure, maintainable, and actually solve the user's problem.
