# Stage 4: Substack repurpose

A Substack post is **not** the transcript cleaned up. People read it, they don't watch it, so it needs a different shape.

## Inputs
The final script, the video's real transcript after filming (use this over the script, because what she actually said beats what was planned), and 3 of her past Substack posts if they exist.

## Shape (800 to 1,500 words)
- **Subject line:** the video title's promise, reworded for the inbox. Don't reuse the video title word for word.
- **Opening:** the most personal moment from the video, told in first person.
- **The thing the video couldn't fit:** a deeper section with the full prompt, the code, the failed attempts, the numbers, or her opinion at length. This is the reason to subscribe. If there's nothing extra to say, the post shouldn't exist, so say so rather than padding it.
- **Embed the video** after the opening, not at the top.
- **Close:** one question readers can reply to by email.

Same hard rules: `delia-voice` spec, zero em dashes, must pass `review/ai_tells.py`. Then run Reviewer A (voice match) on it.
